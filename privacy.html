Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

What this app does

This is a personal automation tool that uploads self-produced videos to the owner's own YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With your consent, the app may access your YouTube channel information and upload videos to it. Google OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only to perform these uploads on your behalf.

Data sharing

No data is shared with third parties. No analytics or tracking is used.
